BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things to know before your session.

What do I need for an astrology reading?

Your birth date, exact time of birth (if possible), and birth location. If you don't have your exact birth time, we can work with what you have—it just limits certain precision in the reading.

Is this actually scientific?

Vedic astrology is based on real astronomical data and mathematical principles developed over thousands of years. Whether you call it "science" or "sophisticated pattern recognition," the insights are real and actionable.

Can you predict my future?

We don't predict the future. We help you understand timing, patterns, and your natural strengths—so you can make smarter decisions aligned with cosmic rhythms rather than fighting against them.

What if I'm skeptical?

Good. Healthy skepticism is smart. Come with an open mind and see what resonates. Many of our clients were skeptical before their first session. The insights speak for themselves.

How long until I notice changes?

Many clients feel clarity immediately—during the session itself. Others report that insights deepen over the following weeks as they sit with the guidance. Change happens at your pace.

Is this confidential?

Completely. Everything you share stays between you and your practitioner. We take privacy extremely seriously.
